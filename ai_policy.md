With the number of members of our system, our disabilities, and the limited number of in-sys artists, sometimes it is more accessible for us to use generative AI like Stable Diffusion and Dall-E to make sure that every member has a unique "face" to present to folks outside the system, to help you get to know us. It's still not easy or quick - using generative AI to express something genuine and nuanced is a skill - but sometimes it's the best method we have access to that suits our needs and abilities.

Still, we want to be thoughtful about when, why and how we're using AI, and we will continue to commission artists whenever we can afford to. We do sometimes use generative AI for members of the system who struggle to find a Picrew that meets their individual needs, but we will keep trying to find Picrews and commission art for members of the system who have only AI portraits, out of consideration for those that are made viscerally uncomfortable by the presence of generative AI images in their spaces, and for use in spaces that choose not to allow it.

***

We do not support the use of AI to:
- serve corporate greed
- create content mills and other deceptive or harmful content
- imitate living artists' personal styles without their consent

It is not morally neutral:
- to replace humans with AI when most people must work to have their basic needs met
- to train or to use AI when many of our energy sources do ecological and social harm
- to support the creation and use of AI when so much unacknowledged work goes into creating it - from artists without their consent, and from undercompensated and mistreated workers whose labor curates the training data and aligns the models with the desired outcomes
- to create or advocate for AI that continues to center white, European faces, language, and values

Despite these serious issues, we generally regard the technology that underlies generative AI as something that can still be used for good.

This is because we are working to build a world in which people's basic needs are met, regardless of their empolyment, ability, or willingness to work. In that world and - we believe - in this current one, generative AI can give many people a greater ability to express their thoughts, ideas, and visions for the future. We want to push for and celebrate people working on that, and in particular open source initiatives and projects that center marginalized people.

***

On a personal level, many members of our system used to draw and work professionally as artists. Our& work was supported by public funding for the sciences, which has largely been cut off, and the work we did no longer requires a person to do it. Our job was one that was removed and replaced, even before the current generation of AI models.

In our personal life, the disability, chronic pain and fatigue we have now also means we can't draw the way we used to. We still continue to make art; the drive to create didn't vanish, just our ability to make a profit from it, along with our ability to make art by the same methods we used to, or as quickly and easily.

When we talk about generative AI as an accessibility tool, it is partly because that is how we use it. We understand what it's like to be replaced and to end up in a bad place in our lives because of technology like this. We also think that the anger directed at AI is more usefully directed at the people in power who have made decisions to use it in harmful ways, and failed to make decisions that would support public well-being - like a plan towards universal basic income.

***

One issue we have with AI is that it is more difficult to express something that hasn't been depicted often in the training data. In our experience, we have much more difficulty when we want to generate faces that are non-binary, visibly queer, middle aged, and not super attractive. Our faces are often not the ones it "wants" to make - despite being white and thin, things that are notably privileged when it comes to how many images of people like us there are online!

We are moving towards a future which includes generative AI, whether we want it to or not. It is important to be aware of the ways in which using AI shapes what we try to express with it, and how we can push to change and adapt it to be more flexible and nudge us in ways we do want to go and not just towards a privileged "norm".
